Laws & Ethics of Brotherhood

Upholding the values, traditions and brotherhood of our Fraternity.

General Conduct

1. Respect & Brotherhood

Treat every brother with dignity, honor, and respect.

2. Integrity & Honesty

Uphold truth, honesty and moral integrity in all dealings inside and outside the lodge.

3. Confidentiality

Keep all lodge matters, rituals and brother's private information confidential.

4. Discipline & Obedience

Respect lodge hierarchy, obey lawful instructions from lodge officers and maintain discipline.
