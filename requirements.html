Initiation Fee
In Freemasonry, initiation fee is required,the purpose of initiation is to symbolically transform a candidate from darkness to light through a ritual that represents death of his old ignorant self and rebirth as a better, moral man, teaching him core values like secrecy, trust, equality and self-discipline through direct experience rather than words, while testing his sincerity and binding him to the fraternity with an oath and introducing the symbolic tools of masonry that will guide his moral development.

Gown / Uniform
The purpose of a uniform is to create equality, identity, discipline and unity by removing outward differences in status, wealth or background and replacing them with a shared appearance that shows belonging to a specific group, role or purpose, while also signaling authority, professionalism and function to outsiders and fostering pride, order and teamwork among those who wear it.

Camel Blood
In Freemasonry, Camel blood is used during initiation; blood is only symbolic, representing life, commitment and the seriousness of the oath where the initiate swears to keep secrets as if his life depends on it, to create a strong moral bond and show that his promise is a blood-level, unbreakable commitment.

Registration Fee
In Freemasonry, the registration fee is a one-time payment required to join that covers lodge expenses, charity and membership materials, plus yearly dues afterward, with the exact amount varying by lodge but intended to show commitment and ensure the candidate is financially stable.
